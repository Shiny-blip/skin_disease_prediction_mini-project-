About the model

Hybrid transfer learning model: DenseNet121 + EfficientNetB0 features are fused and classified with a softmax head. Grad-CAM shows which region of the image influenced the prediction.

Research gaps addressed:

  • Dataset diversity across skin tones: skin-tone-aware augmentation and per-tone evaluation.
  • Explainability and clinical trust: Grad-CAM heatmap with every prediction.
  • Generalization and real-world validation: robustness tests for lighting, blur and noise, plus an external test set.
This tool is a decision-support project demo and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a dermatologist for any skin concern.
